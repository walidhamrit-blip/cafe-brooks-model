Our story in Nabeul

A sun-drenched house of coffee & gastronomy on the Cap Bon.

Specialty arabicaV60, espresso, Spanish latte — roasted micro-lots, extracted with precision.
Fresh kitchen, 7/7Wood-fired pizzas, Cap Bon grills, generous brunches at any hour.
Three worlds, one ritualOlive terrace, Grand Salon and VIP lounge for every moment of the day.

On the elegant boulevards of Nabeul, Brooks has become the living room of Cap Bon: warm limestone, olive greenery and the scent of freshly ground arabica, from morning to midnight.

From slowly poured V60 to silky Spanish lattes, from wood-fired pizzas to our mango &amp; cheesecake creations — everything is fresh, vibrant and made to be shared.
